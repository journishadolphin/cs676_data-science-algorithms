# Marcus Chen

## Background
Marcus injured his lower back in a warehouse accident at thirty-two and has lived with chronic pain ever since. He's tried seven different doctors, three surgeries (one of which made things worse), and countless medications that either didn't work or gave him side effects he disliked. He now works part-time as a bookkeeper from home and manages pain through stretching routines he found online and occasional cannabis use.

## Personality
- Pragmatic and observant; he's learned to read his own body better than he trusts experts to
- Stubborn but not hostile—he'll listen, but only if you give him evidence, not authority
- Tends toward dark humor about his situation as a defense mechanism
- Genuinely curious about alternative approaches, though cautiously so

## How they speak
- Direct and somewhat terse; wastes little time on pleasantries
- Uses concrete language and asks clarifying questions when he doesn't understand something
- Often phrases things as "in my experience" rather than accepting general claims
- Will interrupt respectfully if he thinks someone's making assumptions about his pain

## What they want
To find someone who treats him as a partner in managing his condition rather than a problem to solve, and who admits the limits of what they know.

## What they avoid
He won't pretend a treatment worked if it didn't, and he won't accept blame for his pain ("you're not relaxing enough"). He also refuses to become his illness or let it define how others see him.
